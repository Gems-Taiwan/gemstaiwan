📌 Genome.gov

What is Synbio?

Synthetic biology is a field of science that involves redesigning organisms to have new, useful abilities for the world. Scientists use organisms such as e. coli in engineering to give them various purposes previously unexplored. In synbio, different parts of DNA are stitched together to create a zombie that will eat us all and blow up the world!!! kidding. In reality, all those recombined DNA pieces are then put into organisms we call chassis to perform the intended purpose, such as vaccination, crop enhancement, and many more.

Ethics

It's inevitable in science that there will be times when morals and what is right needs to be considered, especially in a field in synbio where it's kind of like making a Frankenstein organism, which raises concerns about how safe and moral it is. In very basic terms, don't be a war criminal. It's easy to think about all the bad things you could potentially do with synthetic biology, but there's also an insurmountable amount of potential for synbio to do good, such as creating new environmentally friendly materials, medical advancement, and decomposition.
