GEMS Taiwan is a five-year-old team based in Taipei, Taiwan. With two top 10 projects and a Grand Prize, the team is filled with ambition for this year's project as well. Noticing the struggles small farmers encounter when planting crops, such as osmotic, heat, and salinity stress, our team wants to alleviate these concerns through iGEM. Through our education program to other schools, we plan to educate students about the common stresses farmers can face during the year and how crops relate to our daily lives. Our education program plans to target elementary, middle, and high school students.
