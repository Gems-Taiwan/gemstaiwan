What is Synbio?

Synthetic biology is a field of science that involves redesigning organisms to have new, useful abilities for the world. Scientists use organisms such as e. coli in engineering to give them various purposes previously unexplored. In synbio, different parts of DNA are stitched together to create organisms with new capabilities. Those recombined DNA pieces are then put into organisms we call chassis to perform the intended purpose.

Ethics

It's inevitable in science that there will be times when morals and what is right needs to be considered, especially in a field like synthetic biology where we are designing organisms that raises concerns about how safe and moral it is. In very basic terms: don't be a war criminal. All science has the potential to be bad, but it's up to us to make it beneficial instead of detrimental for humanity.
