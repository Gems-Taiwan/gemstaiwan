2024 Workshop
Dengue Fever

Youth Engagement Workshop — Dengue Fever & Synthetic Biology

In our 2024 team workshop, we focus on engaging youth in the fight against dengue fever through different interactive activities, such as bracelet-making, hands-on experiments, and games. And to further promote the topic, dengue fever, we also combine many teaching of synthetic biology.

By breaking down the complex biological concepts, we were able to let students of different ages understand how ATCG, DNA, genes, and cells work. We design the education of synthetic biology to not only spread the idea of synthetic biology, but also ignite the students' interest in synthetic biology.

2025 Workshop
Textile Degradation

Raising Awareness of Textile Pollution Through Interactive Learning

In the 2025 team, we focus on raising awareness of textile pollution through interactive engagement to let the students know more about plastic pollution, textile recycling, and the role of DNA and enzymes in biotechnology.

By planning lessons and interactive activities for different school levels, we were able to evaluate the knowledge suitable for them, adapt our pacing to the students' level, and later quantify how much they have learned. We also combine some synthetic biology into the lessons, through experiments and activities, the students were able to learn more about DNA and synthetic biology.
