Textile Waste Degradation

Enhancing Student Awareness of Textile Pollution Through Interactive Teaching

In our 2025 team, we focus on guiding students to pay attention to textile pollution issues through interactive teaching, leading them to deeply understand plastic pollution, textile recycling, and the critical roles that DNA and enzymes play in biological technology.

By tailoring exclusive courses and interactive activities for students at different educational stages, we can precisely evaluate the knowledge content suitable for their level, flexibly adjust the teaching pace, and quantify their learning outcomes afterward. In addition, we also integrate synthetic biology into our teaching, allowing students to further understand the mysteries of DNA and synthetic biology through experiments and scientific activities.

2024 Workshop
Dengue Fever

Youth Participation Workshop — Dengue Fever & Synthetic Biology

In our 2024 workshop, we committed to engaging youth in actions against Dengue Fever through a variety of activities such as beaded bracelet making, hands-on experiments, and interactive games. To further promote the theme of Dengue Fever prevention and control, we also incorporated fundamental courses in synthetic biology.

By simplifying complex biological concepts, we helped students of different age groups understand the operating mechanisms of ATCG, DNA, genes, and cells. Our synthetic biology education is not only aimed at spreading relevant scientific concepts but also hopes to inspire a profound interest among students in the field of synthetic biology.
